Accessibility rules

focus-visible — 2px --accent ring, 2px offset. Never outline: none without a replacement.
tap targets — 44px minimum on mobile. Buttons are 50px.
status — never colour alone. “live”, “featured”, “archived” are always words.
the whole card is the link, not just the title. One <a> per card.
decorative svg — the hero line, globe and heat grids are aria-hidden; their numbers are in adjacent text.
external links — rel="noopener noreferrer", and the arrow is not the only signal.
contrast — every token on the sheet clears 4.5:1. Do not introduce a darker grey.

Motion rules

Hover changes colour and border only — never position, scale or shadow.
The book spine tilt is the single exception, and it is deliberate.
One signature animation per page load: the hero line. It plays once.
The globe rotation is the only infinite loop, and desktop only.
No count-up numbers, no marquees, no parallax, no scroll-jacking.
prefers-reduced-motion: reduce → hero line renders final-state, globe holds one frame,
spine tilt and skeleton pulse are off. Colour transitions may stay.
